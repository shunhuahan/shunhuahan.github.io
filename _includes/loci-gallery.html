Duplication sizes, identities, and mappability profiles are measured directly from the GRCh38 reference: each gene and its paralog were pulled from NCBI with 2 kb of flanking sequence and aligned, and differences (including indel events) were binned across the aligned homology block. These whole-block identities run lower than the exon-only figures usually quoted for these genes, which is the point: the introns are where reads get lost. Case counts are from the 2026 ACMG platform presentation.
