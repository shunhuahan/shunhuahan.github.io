Simplified, illustrative model, not a real aligner. It assumes independent per-base mismatches at the stated identity, and an aligner that confidently and uniquely places a read only when it has roughly 2% or fewer mismatches to the true locus, which is in the ballpark of common short-read aligner behavior. The core phenomenon it captures is real: past a certain identity, a read genuinely cannot be told apart from its paralog by sequence alone.
