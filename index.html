Sr. Staff Bioinformatics Scientist · Illumina · San Diego

Shunhua Han

I develop computational methods to resolve genetic variation in repetitive and structurally complex regions of the genome. At Illumina I work on copy-aware, haplotype-resolved analysis for TruPath Genome and DRAGEN. My earlier research developed methods to reconstruct transposable element insertions and study genome evolution in Drosophila cell lines.

The through-line is the same in both: when a genome carries several near-identical copies of a sequence, the problem is deciding which copy a read came from — whether that copy is a transposable element insertion, an alpha-globin copy-number configuration, or one of a pair of paralogous genes and its haplotypes.
